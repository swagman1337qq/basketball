Reviewed player cards — November 27, 2026

Includes the later overall/ability follow-up: four further ability refinements
and rookie Lin's potential correction. Six losing proposals were retained only
as rejected experiments in the audit. The earlier review is a historical record.

This set contains the 34 ready-made cards, including Michael Kidd-Gilchrist's
2012–13 rookie build, plus three Charles Barkley season builds.

MKG is now available directly in the game's Player cards menu after refreshing;
existing card libraries receive his template automatically.

The ready-made library updates untouched templates after you refresh the game.
Custom card edits are preserved. To use a downloaded file: open a player in God
Mode, open Player cards, choose Import file, select one JSON card, then Save card
and Apply. Import the individual card, not the card-refinement index file.

Applying changes the player's build. His name, age, team, contract, face and
recorded history stay his own. Existing league players are not automatically
rebuilt by this review; reapply a template to give one the revised build.
Undo restores his previous build, including his college game and translation.

Historical totals and percentages are exact references where the source supplies
them. Some minute targets come from rounded MPG and are marked approximate.
Simulated games vary with teammates, opposition, role, development and chance.
Creation splits that cannot be identified from the source remain estimates.
Small shooting samples are not reliable measures of underlying ability.

KJ Simpson and Blake Griffin have separate college snapshots alongside their
NBA builds. Mudiay's estimated 2021–22 level uses his larger 2018–20 NBA sample.
Lin's 2011–12 reference is his Knicks stint only. Morris's chart excludes playoffs.
