# Team identities — November 13, 2026

The shared TeamLogo renderer now uses 28 selected vintage illustrations, plus the separately approved Los Angeles Stars artwork. Selected images take precedence over saved logo-image/style overrides at every shared logo placement, including old save previews. Full-size source artwork and prompts are archived under `.review/vintage-logos`, `.review/vintage-round-two` and `.review/vintage-round-three`. Production WebP copies preserve the chosen composition and fit without cropping.

## Confirmed names

Philadelphia Liberty, Cleveland Cyclones, Detroit Groove, Nashville Owls, Oakland Panthers, Charlotte Racers, San Jose Electric, San Antonio Valor and Vancouver Cascades. New leagues use these names. Existing saves using prior defaults update the live team and matching historical standings; custom names remain unchanged. San Antonio keeps its existing brown/gold interface palette and Vancouver its existing charcoal/ice palette; the approved artwork retains its own illustrated colors.
