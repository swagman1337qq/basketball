## CBA references

Source: [2023 NBA–NBPA Collective Bargaining Agreement](https://imgix.cosmicjs.com/25da5eb0-15eb-11ee-b5b3-fbd321202bdf-Final-2023-NBA-Collective-Bargaining-Agreement-6-28-23.pdf), supplied through the [NBPA CBA page](https://www.nbpa.com/cba/).

- Article I 1(zz): regular salary excludes signing bonuses and incentives.
- Article VII 3(d), 5(b), 6(h): previous-season performance determines bonus classification; unlikely bonuses are limited to 15% of regular salary, must fit signing funding, and cannot accompany a minimum-exception contract.
- Article VII 4(a)(1)(i), 7(d): waived guaranteed salary stays in team salary; stretch treatment changes after August 31.
- Article VII 5(a): annual raises use first-year salary rather than compounding.
- Article XI 5(b)–(d): offer sheets need funding throughout the pending period and more than one non-option season; the first-year limit for players with one or two service years is the non-taxpayer MLE.
- Article XXIX 2: authorized hardship exceptions can exceed the standard roster count.

These changes address the selected findings. They do not certify the entire game as a complete NBA CBA implementation: F07’s matching hard-cap defect remains open, the compressed calendar and cap tables remain, and the game does not model every possible NBA contract schedule or complex multi-team swap. Existing valid swap rights are preserved by preventing an incompatible later trade; selling a pick does not cancel previously granted legal rights.
