# Trade screen and exclusions — November 13, 2026

The two teams appear in vertically stacked sections. Each has a searchable, sortable roster and a neighboring pick/swap list. The right sidebar summarizes the assets each team receives, payroll after the proposed trade, the salary cap and the change in team overall. It holds the trade actions and league-office results. Narrow layouts stack these sections without forcing the whole page to scroll horizontally. The existing dynamic cap bar remains above the builder.

The first checkbox selects an asset; the X checkbox excludes it from AI package searches. Excluding a selected asset deselects it. Clicking the player name still opens his profile; other player cells toggle selection unless excluded. Exclusions are controlled individually, with no bulk-exclude menus. Age and position remain separate sortable columns and filters. Contract years, PER and current regular-season box-score averages remain visible.

Exclusions are stored by player ID and by owner plus pick/swap ID, so the two teams' same-year swap rights can be controlled separately. They survive filtering, partner changes, clearing selections, and saving/loading. Unchecking an individual X checkbox restores eligibility. Changing exclusions closes old generated offers so a stale package cannot reintroduce an excluded asset.

What would it take retains the currently selected assets and searches the existing package generator for allowed additions. It can add up to three more players and sampled one/two-pick combinations, plus an allowed opponent salary filler. Every suggested package must satisfy the same AI valuation and league-office checks used when proposing the trade. The package search is bounded, not an exhaustive search of every possible combination. If no acceptable legal package is found, the UI reports No deal and leaves the selected offer unchanged. An excluded essential player is never silently restored.

Ask what they want and Shop around league also respect exclusions, including automatic salary fillers. The final proposal handler rejects any selected excluded asset as a defense against stale offers. Contract terms and the CBA rules themselves are unchanged.
