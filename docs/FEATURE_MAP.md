## How it fits together

There's no server. The whole game runs in the browser, saves to IndexedDB and is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages. Almost all of the logic sits in one engine object (`src/engine/Game.ts`). Screens read their values from the view model (`src/ui/viewModel.ts`) and call engine methods for actions; the engine notifies React through `subscribe` / `useSyncExternalStore` (`src/App.tsx`).

## The season loop

Each step is a button on the phase bar (`phase` in `state`). After free agency the loop goes back to training camp.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  Camp["Training camp"] --> Reg["Regular season<br/>82 games"] --> PI["Play-in"] --> PO["Playoffs"] --> Rev["Owner review"] --> Lot["Draft lottery"] --> Dr["Draft"] --> Pre["Pre-Free Agency"] --> FA["Free agency<br/>Jun 30 to Sep 30"] --> Camp
```

| Step | `phase` | What happens | Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Training camp | `preseason` | Summer development (ratings move, ceilings re-estimated, hidden gems surface), retirements, a new class of about 100 prospects, expansion. You cut to 15 standard + 3 two-way contracts. Opening night: short rosters filled with minimum deals, media predictions locked, AI extensions, fire sale if the owner's payroll order is unmet. | `Game.startPreseason()`, `Game.startSeason()`, `progress.ts`, `media.ts` |
| Regular season | `regular` | 82 games, late October to mid-April. Sim a day/week/month/to the deadline/to the end, or watch live. AI teams trade, sign and waive (35% chance a day). Injuries, fatigue, monthly development and scouting reports, mentoring. All-Star Weekend mid-February, trade deadline early February. | `Game.sim()`, `Game.simDay()`, `sim.ts`, `allStar.ts`, `cbaFlow.seasonTick()` |
| Play-in | `playin` | Awards announced. Each conference: 7 v 8, 9 v 10, then the game for the 8 seed. | `Game.startPlayin()`, `Game.simPlayin()`, `awards.ts` |
| Playoffs | `playoffs` | Best-of-7 East and West, then the Finals. When it ends: the owner's year-end letter and the Hall of Fame vote. | `Game.startPlayoffs()`, `Game.simPo()`, `ownerLetter.ts`, `hof.ts` |
| Owner review | `playoffs` → `lottery` | Incentives paid, owner verdicts (you can be fired), your GM contract, the job market. | `frontOffice.seasonReview()`, `gmCareer.ts` |

| Draft | `draft` | Two rounds, 60 picks, rookie-scale deals, draft promises and draft-night heists. ## Feature areas

### Game simulation
- Possession context (2026-11-03): `GameSim` chooses a drive, cut, roll, post, perimeter, transition or putback action before zone selection. A helper may stay home, enter the lane or meet the shooter at the rim. This changes rim access and finish difficulty. `ShotEvent` tracks action, helper and contest. Run `npm run realism -- --shots output.jsonl` for joint player-level volume and efficiency reports that reconcile to the box score.
- Regular-season calendar (2026-11-03): `Game.buildSchedule` staggers balanced rounds across adjacent dates. Teams have 82 games, variable rest and no three games in three days. `calendarV` identifies date-indexed schedules. Older saves adopt the calendar at the next offseason schedule build. `ruleSlot` maps existing rule dates to their equivalent calendar slots. Recovery follows elapsed days, injury games count upcoming team fixtures, and AI rotations respond to short rest. Verified by `scripts/calendar.mts`.
- Rim opportunities (2026-11-03): `rimOpening` and `rimDenial` use creation, help, spacing and workload. NBA and quick CCP resolve the gather (clear, pressured or contested) before selecting a dunk (`finishing.ts` `dunkOpening`, 2026-11-08: a contested gather ends in a dunk only for a real dunker). A closed lane can redirect a preferred rim attempt.
- Finishing execution (2026-11-02): `engine/finishing.ts` shared by NBA/CCP/amateur paths; technique-specific execution and norms, actual subtype counters, zero-capable `ten.dunk`, deterministic event descriptions, joint shooting-foul outcomes and rebounder-owned putbacks. `db.finishV` 1 refreshes norms. Tests: `scripts/finishingKernel.mts`, `finishingPreferences.mts`, `finishingIntegration.mts`. Scope, calibration and limits: `docs/FINISHING_UPDATE_2026-11-02.md`.
- One possession-by-possession engine plays every game, watched or simmed.
- Tuned to the 2025-26 NBA (Basketball-Reference league averages: pace 99.4, 115.6 points, 89.1 FGA, 37.0 3PA, 23.5 FTA, 14.5 TOV; `sim.ts` `BASE`, `RATE`, `CAL`) and to its spread: team scoring about 105–125 (2025-26: 105.9–122.1), team ORtg, eFG%, TOV% and net rating spreads, win totals, single-game margins and home court (about 2.5 points). The spread comes from `TOV_K` (how much a lineup's handling and the defense's pressure move turnovers), `LINEUP_REG` (part of a lineup's shooting edge given back to defensive attention, players' differences intact), `LEAD_K` (a big lead relaxes the leader, urgency lifts the trailer; earlier garbage time), the softer `CURVE` downside, lighter lineup defense and IQ terms, and AI teams' own tempo (`Game.tempoOf`: coach's taste plus roster speed and age, about 97–104 possessions). League norms re-center ratings every season.
- Playing style (`tendencies.ts`, version 4 since 2026-10-08): stored the way the NBA reports it. Location: five shares of all his shots adding up to 100 (Restricted Area, Paint Non-RA, Mid-Range, Corner 3, Above the Break 3). Creation: four shares of his jump shots (mid-range and threes) adding up to 100 (Catch & Shoot, Pull-Up, Stepback, Fadeaway). Usage rate and free throw rate stay 0–100 scores. ## Player value and historical calibration

The possession-engine calibration varied 20 skills and Feel by position group while holding starting rotations and overall ratings fixed. After 75,000 games across three league seeds, the overall calculation blends 60% of the measured shares with 40% of the prior shares and recenters each group's rating scale. In 4,350 separate games, the correlation between the difference in minutes-weighted roster ratings and the game margin moved from 0.652 to 0.653. Height and Defensive IQ carried particularly strong signals. Small or noisy effects retain prior weight.

Coaches use the exact overall and a bounded contribution from season PER after 20 games. Assistant training uses the positional value of skills and the player's available room to improve. Development uses the same skill weights. AI draft boards mix readiness with the team's own upside estimate, and contenders favor readiness more than rebuilders. Trade valuations use the exact overall for present value and growth headroom. Existing saves update overalls, growth plans, league price ranks and upcoming draft boards once on load. Explicit potential overrides keep their saved value.

In the first pass, the 26 historical presets were checked against their real season box scores at historical minutes. NBA cards were tested under their authored style and with AI tendency adjustments. College cards used the amateur stats engine and the appropriate school and year.
