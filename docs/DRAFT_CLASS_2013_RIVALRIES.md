# 2013 draft class: school and club rivalry audit

Reviewed 2026-11-01. Scope: **all 100 prospects**, including every school/club label in the current affiliation and recorded transfer-season history. After normalizing archive aliases, the class uses **102 directed institution pairings: 76 college pairings and 26 club pairings**. Reciprocal substitutions count separately. The table below is the complete institution-level evidence ledger; it contains no real-player name key.

**101 pairings have a documented school rivalry or basketball derby/rivalry. One is a documented competitive-opponent fallback, explicitly approved by the user.** A rivalry does not have to be the institution’s single biggest rivalry. Regional basketball derbies and historical rivalries count; merely being in the same conference or near each other is insufficient. University athletics pages, clubs, leagues and contemporary reporting were preferred. Secondary sources are identified by their links; this is not a claim that every relationship has independent primary-source confirmation. Some formal series names are newer than the historical rivalry itself.

## Corrections

16 prospects have corrected affiliation labels or division metadata; 12 have a changed current affiliation or league label. Names, positions, physical data, personality, ratings, potential, scouting reads and all recorded numerical statistics are unchanged. The 2000 class and engine are unchanged.

- North Texas → UT Arlington, UNC Greensboro → High Point and Arkansas → Texas use documented basketball rivals. San Diego State → UNLV uses a rivalry explicitly described by its coach in January 2013.
- Fresno Pacific → Fresno State replaces the unsupported Azusa Pacific choice; a 2009 official basketball preview calls Fresno Pacific a crosstown rival. The fictional affiliation is NCAA Division I; the recorded numerical line still comes from the original Division II season. Rival substitutions do not turn those source statistics into newly observed rival-team statistics.
- Estudiantes → Real Madrid; Joventut → Barcelona; Valencia → Lucentum Alicante; Pesaro → Fortitudo Bologna. These replace weaker or unsupported selections with documented basketball rivalries/derbies.
- Unicaja → Cajasol Sevilla and Clínicas Rincón → CB Granada keep the two actual historical organizations distinct. **Granada B was unsupported and has been removed.** CB Granada is the senior club from the 2011–12 LEB Oro derby, not the later Fundación/Covirán club or an invented reserve team.
- Flamengo → Brasília: the NBB’s own 2013 article describes this as its biggest classic. Vasco is a genuine historical basketball rival, but its senior team was inactive in 2013, making it a poor affiliation for this class.
- Zaragoza’s rival is named **Peñas Huesca**, with **LEB Oro** rather than ACB. Lucentum Alicante also uses LEB Oro for 2012–13. Quilmes uses **TNA**, the division it played in that season before winning promotion in May 2013.

## Approved fallback

No reliable source establishing a genuine basketball rivalry for Smederevo was found. A contemporary report documents its 2 March 2013 KLS contest against Sloga Kraljevo. The user chose to use a competitive opponent with an explicit fallback label. **Stanimir Stojičić therefore shows “Sloga Kraljevo (opponent fallback)” in the game.** This entry is not counted as a verified rivalry.

## Validation and applying the update

A private source-to-alias worksheet checks every current affiliation and recorded season label against the evidence ledger. The real-player identity key remains ignored and unpublished. Every prospect retains the same non-affiliation fields and numerical history as the previous release. `node --import tsx scripts/historicalClass.mts` checks import/export, save/reopen, all 100 prospect records, the corrected Spanish transfer stints and the explicit fallback across three seeds; `npm run build` performs TypeScript and production compilation.

Refresh the game and load the **2013 draft class** again from God Mode → Draft classes to use the corrections. **Prospects already loaded into a league and previously downloaded/imported copies retain their saved affiliations**; this patch does not rewrite a draft or roster already in progress.
